Publications

Handwritten Devanagari Character Recognition: Conjunct Consonants Dataset Creation and Benchmarking with Transfer Learning

S. Tiwari, S. Khadka, S. Baral, S. P. Panday

Accepted 17th IAPR International Workshop on Document Analysis Systems, 2026

A new benchmark of 50,020 labeled handwritten conjunct consonants, with transfer learning baselines that reach 99.57% on DHCD, above the previous 99%.

Paper Code and dataset

Character Recognition of Nepali Number Plate

S. Khadka, S. Baral, S. Tiwari, S. K. Ghimire

Information Systems for Intelligent Systems, Springer

A two-stage system for Nepali license plates. YOLOv8 extracts the plate and detects characters, then a CNN recognizes each one.

Paper Code

Selected projects

  • GPT-2 from scratch

    A GPT-2 model built from the ground up and trained on the Tiny Shakespeare dataset.

    Transformers, language modeling

  • Niti, a legal question answering chatbot

    Semantic search over legal texts so people can ask legal questions in plain language. Winner of HackADev 2023, organized by UNDP, Citra Labs, Diyo.AI and NAAMII.

    NLP, semantic search

  • Diabetes readmission prediction

    Random forest and logistic regression models that predict 30-day readmission for diabetic patients (AUC 0.7036), with a fairness evaluation across age, race and gender subgroups.

    Machine learning, fairness

  • Nepali language auto-completion

    An LSTM-based next-word suggestion tool for Nepali, trained on a public corpus.

    LSTM, Nepali NLP

  • Skin cancer classification on ISIC

    Transfer learning for skin lesion classification on the ISIC dataset.

    PyTorch, medical imaging

  • Support vector machine from scratch

    A NumPy implementation on the Iris dataset, written as a guide to the mathematics and optimization behind SVMs.

    NumPy, optimization
